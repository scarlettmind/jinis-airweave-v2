Growing up

Growing up, one night at a time.

Children arrive at JINIS at six and leave at twelve. In between, they grow taller, stronger and more independent, often faster than anyone expects.

airweave grows with them. Its mattresses are made in sections, and each zone (shoulders, hips, legs) can be adjusted softer or firmer. As a child’s body changes, the bed can be re-fitted to the child who sleeps in it.
